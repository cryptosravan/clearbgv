2. Important Clauses to Review

These are the areas ClearBGV should flag for your attention.

💰 Compensation & Variable Pay

Check fixed pay, variable pay, bonuses, deductions and payment conditions.

📅 Joining Date & Conditions

Review joining deadline, pre-joining conditions, document requirements and contingencies.

⏳ Probation & Notice Period

Check probation duration, confirmation criteria and notice-period requirements.

📍 Work Location & Mobility

Review office location, relocation expectations, travel and transfer clauses.

🔒 Confidentiality & IP

Understand confidentiality, intellectual property and information-security obligations.

🚪 Termination & Exit Terms

Review termination conditions, notice, recovery clauses and post-employment obligations.
